## Solicitation scams — ignore these

Because USPTO filings are public, the application data (name, address,
phone, status, and the full Office Action PDF) is downloadable by anyone
from tsdr.uspto.gov. Scammers scrape it and make contact.

Seen so far:

- **Text from +1 (650) 770-8892**, "Santiago" / "Trademarks Experts" —
  claimed another party is filing for ICE READY. **False.** A 2(d)
  refusal cites an existing *registration*, never a competing applicant.
  Also omitted the 2(a) refusal entirely.
- **Email from trevorcaudle@tclplawpartners.com** — flat-fee Office
  Action solicitation. Marked as spam.

Rule of thumb: never hire anyone who contacted you first about this
filing.
